GOOD QUESTIONS

Solar FAQs.

Useful answers before you commit.

Will solar completely eliminate my utility bill?

Not necessarily. Fixed charges, nighttime usage, seasonal production, and local export-credit rules may leave you with a bill even if you produce a large share of your electricity.

Is the federal 30% residential solar credit still available in 2026?

The former U.S. residential clean energy credit is not available for expenditures after December 31, 2025. State, local, and utility incentives may still exist. Review current rules with a qualified tax professional and use IRS guidance.

Will solar panels keep my house running during an outage?

Most standard grid-tied installations automatically shut off during a grid outage for safety. Backup usually requires a compatible inverter and storage or other approved backup design.

How accurate is this estimator?

It is a deliberately simplified planning tool. It uses assumed peak-sun hours and system losses rather than location-specific weather, roof geometry and utility tariff models. Treat it as a conversation starter, not an installation proposal.
